FAQ about anxiety & seizure disorder medicines

What medicines are used for anxiety and seizure disorders?

Doctors may prescribe anti-anxiety medicines, antidepressants, beta blockers, or anti-seizure medicines depending on the patient’s condition and medical history.

Can anxiety medicines also help with seizures?

Some medicines may be used for both anxiety symptoms and seizure control, but they should only be taken under medical supervision.

Are anxiety and seizure medicines safe for long-term use?

Some treatments are suitable for long-term use, while others may cause dependence, tolerance, or withdrawal effects if used for too long.

Can I buy anxiety and seizure disorder medicines online in the UK?

Yes, but prescription medicines should only be bought from a trusted online pharmacy with proper prescription checks and medical approval.

Can I stop taking seizure or anxiety medicine suddenly?

No, stopping suddenly may increase seizure risk, worsen anxiety, or cause withdrawal symptoms, so always follow a doctor’s advice.
