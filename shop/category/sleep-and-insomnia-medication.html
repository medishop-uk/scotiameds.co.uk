FAQ about sleep & insomnia medicines

What medicines are commonly used for sleep problems?

Doctors may prescribe short-term sleep medicines, sedative treatments, or other options depending on the cause of insomnia and the patient’s health condition.

Can I buy insomnia medicines online in the UK?

Yes, but prescription sleep medicines should only be requested from a trusted online pharmacy with proper medical checks and approval.

Are sleep medicines safe to take every night?

Some sleep medicines are only recommended for short-term use because regular use may increase the risk of tolerance, dependence, or side effects.

How quickly do insomnia medicines work?

Many sleep medicines may start working within a short time, but the exact timing depends on the medicine, dose, and individual response.

Can I stop taking sleep medicine suddenly?

Certain sleep medicines should not be stopped suddenly without medical advice, as this may cause withdrawal symptoms or rebound insomnia.
