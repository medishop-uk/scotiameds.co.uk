FAQ about anxiety and panic disorder medicines

What medicines are commonly used for anxiety and panic disorder?

Doctors may prescribe antidepressants, beta blockers, or short-term calming medicines depending on the patient’s symptoms, health history, and treatment needs.

Can anxiety medicine help with panic attacks?

Yes, some prescribed medicines can help reduce the frequency and severity of panic attacks when used under proper medical supervision.

How long does anxiety medicine take to work?

Some medicines may take a few weeks to show full results, while fast-acting treatments may work sooner for short-term symptom relief.

Are panic disorder medicines safe for daily use?

Some medicines are suitable for regular use, but others should only be taken short term because they may cause dependence or side effects.

Can I stop taking anxiety medicine suddenly?

No, stopping suddenly may cause withdrawal symptoms or make anxiety worse, so patients should always follow their doctor’s advice.
