Side effects of Sedil 5 mg

Like all medicines, Sedil 5 mg may cause side effects, and not everyone will experience them in the same way. Patients should read the patient information leaflet and speak with a doctor or pharmacist if they feel unwell or unsure.

  • Drowsiness and tiredness: Diazepam can make users sleepy, less alert, or slower to react.
  • Dizziness or poor coordination: Some people may feel unsteady, confused, or have difficulty concentrating.
  • Mood changes: Rarely, diazepam may cause agitation, irritability, low mood, or unusual behavior.
  • Breathing risk: Taking diazepam with alcohol, opioids, sleeping tablets, or other sedatives can increase serious breathing-related risks.
  • Dependence and withdrawal: Regular or long-term use may lead to tolerance, dependence, and withdrawal symptoms if stopped suddenly.
