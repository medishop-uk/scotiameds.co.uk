Side effect of alprazolam

Alprazolam may cause side effects, and the reaction can be different from person to person. The risk can increase if the medicine is taken in a higher dose, used for too long, or mixed with alcohol or other sedatives.

  • Mood changes, irritability, low motivation, or unusual behavior may happen.
  • Long-term or incorrect use may lead to tolerance, dependence, or withdrawal symptoms.
  • Urgent help is needed for breathing problems, severe allergic reactions, extreme sedation, or suicidal thoughts.
