Side effects of zopiclone

Zopiclone can help with short-term sleep problems when prescribed correctly, but it may cause side effects and should not be mixed with alcohol or other sedatives. If side effects are severe, unusual, or do not go away, speak with a doctor or pharmacist for advice.

  • Dry mouth or a bitter, metallic taste in the mouth.
  • Feeling tired, sleepy, dazed, or less alert the next day.
  • Dizziness, poor coordination, or reduced concentration.
  • Sleepwalking or doing activities while not fully awake.
  • Serious warning signs may include breathing difficulty, allergic reaction, hallucinations, low mood, or thoughts of self-harm.

FAQ about Zopiclone

What is zopiclone used for?

Zopiclone is used as a short-term treatment for insomnia when sleep problems are affecting daily life. It may help people fall asleep faster, reduce night waking, and improve sleep quality when prescribed by a doctor.

Is zopiclone 7.5 mg a sleeping tablet?

Yes, zopiclone 7.5 mg is commonly known as a sleeping tablet. It belongs to a group of medicines called non-benzodiazepine hypnotics and is usually prescribed for short-term sleep difficulties.

Can I buy zopiclone online in the UK?

How long does zopiclone take to work?

Zopiclone usually starts working within about one hour, so it is commonly taken shortly before bedtime. It should only be taken when you can have a full night’s sleep and follow your prescriber’s instructions.

Is zopiclone addictive?

Zopiclone can cause dependence, especially if used for longer than recommended or taken at higher doses. Doctors usually prescribe it for short-term use to reduce the risk of tolerance, dependence, and withdrawal symptoms.

What should I avoid while taking zopiclone?

Avoid alcohol, driving, operating machinery, and taking other sedative medicines unless your doctor says it is safe. Alcohol can increase drowsiness and may make side effects more dangerous.

What are the common side effects of zopiclone?

Common side effects may include a bitter or metallic taste, dry mouth, daytime sleepiness, dizziness, tiredness, and reduced concentration. Serious symptoms such as breathing difficulty, allergic reaction, hallucinations, or unusual behaviour need urgent medical advice.
