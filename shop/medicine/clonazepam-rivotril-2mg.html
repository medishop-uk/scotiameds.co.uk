Side effects of Clonazepam

Clonazepam can help when prescribed correctly, but it can also cause side effects, especially when taken at the wrong dose or mixed with alcohol or sedatives. Some side effects are mild, while others need urgent medical attention, so patients should read the leaflet and speak with a doctor or pharmacist if symptoms continue.

  • Daytime sleepiness, drowsiness, tiredness, or reduced alertness.
  • Dizziness, light-headedness, unsteadiness, or poor coordination.
  • Disturbed sleep, vivid dreams, memory difficulty, or confusion.
  • Muscle weakness, mood changes, irritability, restlessness, or unusual behavior.
  • Serious warning signs include breathing difficulty, hallucinations, yellowing of the eyes or skin, racing heartbeat, swelling of the lips or throat, or a severe allergic reaction.

FAQ about clonazepam

What is clonazepam used for?

Clonazepam is used to control seizures or fits due to epilepsy, involuntary muscle spasms, panic disorder, and sometimes restless legs syndrome. The exact reason for use should always be confirmed by a doctor.

Is Revotril 2 mg the same as clonazepam?

Revotril 2 mg is a brand-style product name used for clonazepam 2 mg. The active ingredient is clonazepam, but patients should always check the packaging, leaflet, strength, and pharmacy guidance before use.

Can I buy clonazepam online in the UK?

Clonazepam can only be supplied through a legal prescription-led process. Any website offering clonazepam without a prescription or safety check should be avoided because the medicine may be unsafe, counterfeit, or unsuitable.

How long does clonazepam take to work?

The working time depends on the condition being treated. NHS guidance says clonazepam may take around one hour to start working for panic disorder and restless legs syndrome, while seizure or muscle spasm treatment may be adjusted gradually.

Is clonazepam addictive?

Clonazepam can cause dependence, especially if used for a long time or taken outside medical instructions. If treatment needs to stop, the dose should usually be reduced gradually under medical supervision.

Can I drink alcohol with Revotril 2 mg?

No, alcohol should be avoided while taking clonazepam. Alcohol can increase sedation and may make breathing, alertness, and waking from sleep more difficult.

When should I seek urgent help after taking clonazepam?

Seek urgent medical help if you have breathing difficulty, severe drowsiness, confusion, fainting, swelling of the lips or throat, blue or pale skin, hallucinations, chest symptoms, or a serious allergic reaction.
