FAQ about Noctin 5mg

What is Noctin 5mg used for?

Noctin 5mg is prescribed for the short-term treatment of severe insomnia when sleep problems significantly affect daily functioning.

Is nitrazepam legal in the UK?

Yes. Nitrazepam is legal in the UK when prescribed by a qualified healthcare professional and obtained through regulated channels.

How long does nitrazepam take to work?

The sedative effects may last several hours, and, in some people, residual drowsiness can continue into the following day.

Is nitrazepam a sleeping tablet?

Nitrazepam is not primarily a sleeping tablet. However, because it has calming properties, some people may experience drowsiness.

Can I take nitrazepam every day?

Nitrazepam should only be taken exactly as prescribed. Long-term use may increase the risk of tolerance and dependence.

Can nitrazepam cause addiction?

Yes. nitrazepam belongs to the benzodiazepine family and may become habit-forming if used improperly or for extended periods.

What should I avoid while taking nitrazepam?

Patients should avoid alcohol, recreational substances, and activities requiring full alertness, such as driving, until they understand how the medication affects them.
