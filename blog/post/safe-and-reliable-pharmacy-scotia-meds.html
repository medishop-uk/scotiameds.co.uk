The importance of avoiding counterfeit medicines

One of the biggest issues related to modern day pharmacy is the availability of counterfeit medicines specially among the unregistered and unauthorised online sellers. As a result, people are often concerned about the risk of receiving counterfeit of fake drugs when they purchase products from online platforms.

People who are using Scotia Meds, are able to reduce the risk of counterfeit medicines as it is a trusted online pharmacy service provider who is strictly following all their healthcare regulations.

Some of the common signs to identify counterfeit medicines or fake drugs are –

·       Lack of batch number and expiry dates

·       Improper packaging quality.

·       Prescription based medicines are sold without prescriptions

·       Lack of manufacturing details

Fake or counterfeit medicines can be very dangerous for people as they do not contain necessary ingredients or sometimes include unsafe substances that can cause harm to human health.

Risks of buying from unverified websites

People are required to be cautious about purchasing medicine from online platforms. Unregulated and unregistered pharmacy service-based websites may engage in selling counterfeit medicines that are harmful for human health. Potential risks associated to counterfeit medicines are –

·       Increasing antibiotic resistance

Using trusted online medicine service providers like Scotia Meds can help people to reduce these risks.
