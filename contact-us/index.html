Common contact questions

How can I ask about an existing order?

Message us on WhatsApp or Telegram with your order reference and a short description of your question.

Can I check availability before ordering?

Yes. Send the medicine name, strength and pack size you are interested in, and ask the team to confirm current availability and the next steps.

Where can I ask about delivery?

Use either contact option above and include your delivery area. The team can confirm the available delivery options for your enquiry.
