Do I need a prescription to send a request with ScotiaMeds?

Yes. Under UK law, all prescription-only medicines require a valid NHS or private prescription. You can provide your prescription during the request process, and our pharmacy team will review it before approval.

How long does delivery take?

After your prescription has been verified, medicines are usually dispatched the same day. Delivery is sent through tracked Royal Mail or DPD services and commonly arrives within 1–2 working days across the UK.

Is the packaging discreet?

Yes. Every ScotiaMeds request is shipped in plain, unbranded packaging. There are no visible pharmacy or medicine details on the parcel, helping ensure your privacy.

Are your medicines genuine and approved for use in the UK?

Yes. We source medicines only from authorised UK wholesalers regulated by the MHRA. All products are genuine, sealed, and dispensed by UK-registered pharmacists.

Can I contact a pharmacist for guidance?

Yes. Our UK-registered pharmacists can help with questions about dosage, side effects, interactions, and the safe use of your prescribed treatment. Contact our support team through WhatsApp or Telegram.
