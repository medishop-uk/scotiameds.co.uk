Ask on WhatsApp
Do I need a prescription?

Yes. Prescription-only medicines require a valid prescription and checks before approval or dispatch.

How do I place an order?

Add your preferred medicine and pack, then send the prepared request using WhatsApp or Telegram.

How long does delivery take?

Timing depends on approval, availability and service. We confirm an estimate before completion.

Is packaging discreet?

Yes. Orders are sent in plain external packaging without medicine details outside.

Where do you deliver?

Across the UK, subject to eligibility and address checks.
