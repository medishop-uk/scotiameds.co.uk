Safe online pharmacy access can make the process smoother. Some key health facts in Scotland

21% of adults show signs of a possible psychiatric disorder
According to the Scottish Health Survey 2023, around one in five adults met the threshold for a possible psychiatric condition.

Mental health conditions have increased significantly since 2011
Census data shows the proportion of people reporting a mental health condition rose from 4.4% in 2011 to 11.3% in 2022

Young adults face the greatest mental health burden
Adults aged 16–34 report the highest levels of anxiety, self-harm, and previous suicide attempts compared with older age groups.

Depression affects around 15% of GP-registered patients
Public Health Scotland data indicates that approximately 15 out of every 100 registered patients have a recorded diagnosis of depression.

Suicide rates are improving but remain a concern
Scotland recorded 704 probable suicide deaths in 2024, an 11% decrease from 2023 and the lowest figure since 2017. However, suicide rates remain substantially higher among men and in the most deprived communities.

FAQ about online pharmacies

Is an online pharmacy safe to use in Scotland?

Yes, an online pharmacy can be safe when it follows UK pharmacy rules, uses proper prescription checks, and provides clear medicine guidance. Customers should avoid websites that offer prescription-only medicines without review.

Can I buy prescription medicine online in the UK?

You can order prescription medicine online, but it should only be supplied after a valid prescription or suitable clinical assessment. This protects customers from unsafe or unsuitable medicine use.

Do online pharmacies provide discreet delivery?

Many online pharmacies provide discreet delivery to protect customer privacy. This is useful for sensitive health concerns such as anxiety, sleep problems, or prescription-based treatment needs.

What information is needed to order medicine online?

You may need to provide your symptoms, medical history, allergies, current medicines, previous dosage, and prescription details. Accurate information helps the pharmacy assess whether the medicine is suitable.

Can I order anxiety, sleep, or stimulant medicines online?

You can order eligible anxiety, sleep, or stimulant medicines online, but these treatments often need strict prescription and safety checks. Approval depends on clinical suitability, legal requirements, and the information you provide.
