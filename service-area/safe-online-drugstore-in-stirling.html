Can I buy prescription medicine from an online pharmacy?

Yes, prescription medicines can be supplied online when proper pharmacy rules are followed. The GPhC says its register is the only way to confirm whether a pharmacy is legally operating in Great Britain and meeting safety standards.

Is it safe to order sleeping or anxiety medicine online?

It can be safe when the order is handled through a responsible pharmacy process with the right checks. Sleeping and anxiety medicines should not be used casually, and prescription-only medicines should only be taken when suitable for the patient.

How do I know if an online pharmacy is genuine?

A genuine online pharmacy should provide clear medicine information, proper contact details, and registration information. Customers should avoid websites that sell prescription medicines without checks, make unrealistic claims, or offer suspiciously cheap medicines.
