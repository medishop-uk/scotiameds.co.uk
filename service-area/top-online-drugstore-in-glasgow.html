FAQ about online drugstore

Is it safe to buy medicine from an online drugstore?

Yes, as long as the pharmacy is licensed and follows proper regulations. Always check for verification and secure payment options before ordering.

Do I need a prescription to order medication online?

For prescription-only medicines, a valid prescription from a licensed doctor is required. Over-the-counter products can be purchased without one.

How long does delivery usually take?

Delivery times vary by location and service, but most online drugstores offer fast shipping within a few days. Some also provide next-day delivery options.

Are online pharmacies cheaper than local pharmacies?

Often yes, because online stores have lower overhead costs. This allows them to offer competitive pricing and discounts on many medicines.

Is my personal information kept private?

Reputable online pharmacies use encrypted systems to protect your data. They also ensure discreet packaging and confidential handling of orders.[c]
