Can I buy prescription medicine from an online pharmacy?

Yes, prescription medicines can be supplied online only when proper rules are followed. The NHS advises people to check that an online pharmacy is registered with the General Pharmaceutical Council before buying medicines online.

Is it safe to order anxiety or sleeping medicine online?

It can be safe when the medicine is supplied through a responsible pharmacy process with proper checks. Anxiety and sleeping medicines should never be used casually, and prescription-only medicines must be taken only when suitable for the patient.

How do I know if an online pharmacy is genuine?

A genuine online pharmacy should provide clear contact details, medicine information, and registration information. The GPhC warns that unregistered websites may sell medicines that are fake, unsafe, or not checked by a pharmacist.

Why should Inverness customers use an online pharmacy?

An online pharmacy can save time, improve privacy, and make medicine access easier for people with busy routines or repeat treatment needs. It is helpful for customers who prefer home delivery instead of visiting a physical pharmacy.
