Edinburgh: A city with real health priorities

Scotland's capital is home to more than 550,000 people, with a population that spans all age groups and health needs. Understanding the city's health landscape helps us serve you better.

Cardiovascular disease accounts for around 35% of all deaths in Scotland, making heart medication management a daily reality for thousands of Edinburgh households.

Diabetes prevalence in Edinburgh sits at approximately 6.5% of the adult population, driving consistent demand for insulin, metformin, and blood glucose monitoring supplies.

Mental health conditions — including anxiety and depression — affect an estimated 1 in 4 Edinburgh adults each year, with antidepressant prescribing rates in Lothian among the highest in Scotland.

Respiratory conditions such as asthma and COPD affect over 80,000 people across the Lothian region, requiring reliable access to inhalers and bronchodilators year-round.

Edinburgh's aging population (over 20% aged 65+) means polypharmacy—managing multiple medications—is increasingly common, and convenient home delivery is not a luxury but a necessity.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use my NHS prescription with an online pharmacy in Edinburgh? 

Yes. ScotiaMeds is registered to dispense NHS prescriptions across Scotland. You can send your paper prescription by post or use the NHS Electronic Prescription Service (EPS) if your GP practice is set up for it.

How quickly can I receive my medication in Edinburgh? 

Most Edinburgh requests placed before 2 pm qualify for next-day delivery. Same-day dispatch is available for select postcodes.

Is it safe to request prescription medicine online? 

It is completely safe when you use a GPhC-registered pharmacy. ScotiaMeds is fully registered and regulated.

What types of medication does ScotiaMeds supply? 

We supply a full range of NHS and private prescription medicines, over-the-counter (OTC) products, vitamins and supplements, and medical devices.

How do I transfer my repeat prescription to ScotiaMeds? 

Transferring is straightforward. Simply register with us online and nominate ScotiaMeds as your preferred pharmacy through your GP practice or via the NHS app.
