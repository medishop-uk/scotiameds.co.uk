About Us — ScotiaMeds

ScotiaMeds is a fully accredited online pharmacy dedicated to providing safe, discreet, and clinician-approved healthcare to patients across Scotland and throughout the United Kingdom. We were founded on a firm belief that access to quality treatment should not be dictated by where you live, how long you can wait, or whether you feel comfortable walking into a clinic. For many people in Scotland, specialist care and timely prescriptions remain unnecessarily hard to reach — ScotiaMeds is here to bridge that gap.

Each prescription is authorised by a UK-registered prescriber and fulfilled by our GPhC-accredited dispensing team. Everything we do is grounded in trust, clinical rigour, and a genuine commitment to patient wellbeing.

Our work process

Our end-to-end process is built for speed, safety, and simplicity — from your first click to your front door.

  1. Complete your consultation: Fill in a brief medical questionnaire covering your health background and treatment goals. The whole process takes under five minutes.
  1. Clinician review: A GMC-registered doctor carefully assesses your responses and confirms whether the treatment is clinically appropriate for your individual needs.
  1. Pharmacy dispensing: Our GPhC-registered dispensing team prepares your medication with thorough quality checks and accurate clinical labeling.
  1. Delivered to your door: requests are dispatched in plain, unmarked packaging. Same-day and next-day tracked delivery options are available across the UK.

What sets us apart

ScotiaMeds is more than a digital dispensary — we are a team of regulated clinicians and pharmacists who take personal responsibility for the outcomes of every patient we serve.

  • Fully accredited & regulated: ScotiaMeds is registered with the GPhC and subject to MHRA regulation, ensuring full compliance with all UK pharmacy law and clinical governance requirements.
  • Clinician-led prescribing: Each consultation is personally reviewed by a GMC-registered doctor with relevant clinical experience. We do not rely on automation to make prescribing decisions.
  • Uncompromising patient privacy: Your personal and medical data is protected by robust encryption and is never sold or shared. Deliveries are dispatched in plain, anonymous packaging to preserve your confidentiality.
  • Scotland-wide and national delivery: requests placed before 3pm are dispatched the same day. Next-day tracked delivery is available throughout Scotland, England, and Wales.
  • Verified, licensed medications: All medicines are sourced through MHRA-approved supply chains, guaranteeing authenticity, quality, and full regulatory compliance.
  • Ongoing support beyond dispensing: Our clinical team is here for the long haul. Follow-up queries after treatment begins are always welcomed and responded to by qualified staff.
