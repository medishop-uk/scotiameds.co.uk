How to check fake medicines

Fake medicines can be dangerous because they may contain the wrong ingredient, wrong strength, or harmful substances. Before buying sleeping tablets online, always check the seller and the medicine details carefully.

  • Check whether the online pharmacy has clear contact information.
  • Avoid websites that sell prescription medicine with no checks.
  • Be careful with very cheap prices or unrealistic claims.
  • Check packaging, spelling, batch number, and expiry date.
  • Do not buy from random social media sellers.
  • Use official pharmacy register checks where possible.
  • Stop using any medicine that looks damaged, unusual, or suspicious.

FAQ about sleeping pills in the United Kingdom

Can I buy sleeping pills online in the UK?

Yes, some sleeping pills can be requested online, but prescription-only sleeping tablets must follow proper rules. A responsible online pharmacy should check suitability before supplying medicines that may cause side effects or dependence.

What are sleeping pills used for?

Sleeping pills are usually used for short-term sleep problems when insomnia is severe or other steps have not worked. They may help people fall asleep or stay asleep, but they should not be used casually or for long-term sleep management without medical advice.

Are sleeping pills safe?

Sleeping pills can be helpful for some people, but they can also cause side effects such as drowsiness, dizziness, memory problems, tolerance, and dependence. This is why they should be used only as directed and usually for a short period.

How do I know if an online pharmacy is genuine?

A genuine online pharmacy should provide clear business details, medicine information, proper checks, and safe dispensing standards. Avoid any website that offers strong sleeping pills without asking for prescription or health information.
